# Degree Roadmap — Doctor of Public Policy (D.P.P.), Economic Policy Cognate

**Institution:** Liberty University
**Total Credits Required:** 60 c.h.
**Target Graduation:** Fall 2029
**Program Entry:** Fall 2026 (Age 41) → Capstone IV Fall 2029 (Age 44)
**Degree plan effective:** Fall 2026–Summer 2027 cohort (per Liberty's published degree plan for students starting in this window)

> **Correction (2026-10-16):** This program was initially assumed to be a traditional Ph.D. with a dissertation. It was confirmed to actually be a **Doctor of Public Policy (D.P.P.)** — a professional/applied doctorate culminating in a **Capstone Project**, not a dissertation. The course sequence below reflects Liberty's official, published D.P.P. degree plan. See [program_info/dpp_program_notes.md](../program_info/dpp_program_notes.md) for the PhD-vs-DPP/dissertation-vs-capstone analysis.

## Graduation Requirements
- Complete 60 credit hours
- Max 50% transfer credit allowed (with approval), including credit from an earned degree from Liberty University at the same academic level
- Minimum 3.0 GPA; no grade below B- counts toward the degree
- Successful completion of Research Concept (PLCY 885)
- Successful completion of the Capstone sequence (PLCY 887–890), per the Helms School of Government Capstone Handbook
- Degree completed within **5 years** of admission
- Degree Completion Application submitted in the final semester

## Strategy Note
Every course assignment where the syllabus allows topic choice should be steered toward the capstone focus area (healthcare digital infrastructure & data-driven public policy in emerging economies) to build a continuous body of work feeding directly into the Research Concept (PLCY 885) and the Capstone I–IV sequence (PLCY 887–890).
