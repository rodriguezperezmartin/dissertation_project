# Degree Roadmap — Ph.D. in Public Policy (Economic Policy Cognate)

**Institution:** Liberty University
**Total Credits Required:** 60 c.h.
**Target Graduation:** Fall 2029
**Program Entry:** Fall 2026 (Age 41) → Defense Fall 2029 (Age 44)

## Graduation Requirements
- Complete 60 credit hours
- Max 50% transfer credit allowed (with approval)
- Minimum 3.0 GPA; no grade below B- counts toward the degree
- Successful completion of Research Concept (PLCY 885)
- Successful dissertation defense (PLCY 990)
- Degree completed within 7 years of admission
- Degree Completion Application submitted in final semester

## Strategy Note
Every course assignment where the syllabus allows topic choice should be steered toward the dissertation focus area (healthcare digital infrastructure & data-driven public policy in emerging economies) to build a continuous body of work feeding directly into the Research Concept (PLCY 885) and Dissertation I–IV.
