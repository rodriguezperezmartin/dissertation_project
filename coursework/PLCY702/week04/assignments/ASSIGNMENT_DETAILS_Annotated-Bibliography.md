## Official Assignment Requirements

### Part 1: Research Interest Statement

Write a **complete but concise paragraph** (aim for 150–200 words) that includes:

- **Public policy issue you intend to research** for your dissertation or capstone project
- **Research question** (clear and specific, connecting to founding-era constitutionalism)
- **Doctoral program and cognate area** (e.g., "Doctor of Philosophy in Public Policy, Cognate: Healthcare Policy")
- **Explanation of consistency** — how your research interest/question align with your chosen program and cognate
- **Constitutional angle** (required by this course) — which constitutional principles, precedents, or considerations are relevant to your policy area?

**Capstone-Specific Guidance:**
Frame your research interest around **healthcare digital infrastructure in emerging economies** with a constitutional lens. Example research questions:
- "How do federalism principles and constitutional limits on administrative power shape digital health infrastructure adoption in emerging democracies?"
- "To what extent do traditional constitutional protections of local autonomy explain different healthcare policy choices in Guatemala vs. Mexico?"
- "What role do constitutional protections of property rights and due process play in shaping resistance to centralized health data mandates in emerging economies?"

---

### Part 2: Annotated Bibliography (At Least 10 Sources)

**Source Requirements:**
- All sources must be **peer-reviewed academic sources** from scholarly journals, university presses, or academic databases
- All sources must be **clearly relevant to the policy issue** you defined in Part 1
- Minimum 10 sources (more is acceptable)

**Format for Each Source:**
1. **APA 7 citation** (properly formatted)
2. **Annotation: 200–250 words** covering:
   - **Central thesis or research question** of the source
   - **Methodology/approach** — how does the author support their thesis or answer their research question?
   - **Key findings** — what are the main conclusions?
   - **Relevance/applicability** — how does this source address constitutional principles relevant to YOUR research question? How does it inform your capstone/policy research?

**Organization:**
- Sources must be **alphabetized by author's last name**
- Full annotation appears **beneath each citation** (not in separate section)
- See [REFERENCE_Sample-Annotated-Bibliography-graduate.pdf](REFERENCE_Sample-Annotated-Bibliography-graduate.pdf) for Liberty University's official formatting example
