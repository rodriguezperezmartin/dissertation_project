## Requirements for All Replies

- **Number:** Exactly 3 replies to 3 different classmates' threads
- **Word count:** Minimum 250 words per reply (aim for 275–325)
- **Sources:** At least 2 scholarly sources per reply in APA 7 format
- **Analytical method:** "Extend, Contrast, or Challenge"
  - **Extend:** Build on their argument; add a nuance or evidence they missed
  - **Contrast:** Point out where their interpretation differs from another source
  - **Challenge:** Respectfully probe a logical weakness or evidentiary gap
- **Tone:** Substantive, respectful, intellectually rigorous — never superficial
