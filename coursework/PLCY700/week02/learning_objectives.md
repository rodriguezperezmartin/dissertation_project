# Week 2 — Learning Objectives & Study Guide

## Overview
Scholarly writing is much different in purpose and scope than other forms of writing. Scholarly analysis is done utilizing the scientific method and is impartial in its approach. A scholarly author does not have an agenda or bias and seeks only to draw the conclusions presented to him or her from the data.

As a budding scholar, you must evaluate and understand what sets scientific, scholarly writing and analysis apart from other forms of writing so that you can think and write in an impartial and scientific way.

**Core Learning Activity:** Write a Short Paper (1,250–1,750 words, APA format) comparing an academic article against a popular press article using Walliman's scientific inquiry concepts (Popper, Kuhn, Feyerabend), develop 2 scientific research questions, and assess the scientific method from a biblical worldview.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon completion of this module, you will be able to:
- Distinguish scientific/scholarly inquiry from popular press writing using Walliman's epistemological framework.
- Apply Popper's falsifiability, Kuhn's paradigm shifts, and Feyerabend's epistemological anarchism to real-world articles.
- Relate an article to a specific social research approach (e.g., positivist, interpretivist).
- Develop sound, scientific, falsifiable research questions.
- Assess the scientific method from a Biblical worldview.

### Overview

### Instructions
Your Short Paper Assignment has two sections:

**Section 1 – Article Review:**
Using the concepts from Walliman, Chapter 2 and this week's readings on public policy,
- Locate 1 academic article and 1 popular press article in your policy area of interest.
- Compare and contrast the articles using the scientific inquiry concepts from Walliman.
  - Why is the popular press article not considered scientific inquiry?
  - What specific criteria make the journal article scientific inquiry?
  - Include the ideas of Popper, Kuhn, and Feyerabend in your analysis.
- Relate your article to a specific approach to social research outlined in Walliman (e.g. positivist, interpretivist, etc.) and explain how your article fits within the approach you choose.

**Section 2 – Research Questions:**
Once you have read and reviewed these articles, develop 2 sound research questions related to the topic of your articles using the research question criteria presented in this week's module. Explain how each question is scientific in nature and appropriate for scientific research.

**Section 3 – Biblical Worldview:**
Assess the use of the scientific method from a Biblical perspective, citing the module's learn items in your assessment. Where did the scientific method originate? What does Scripture call us to do as we evaluate both the social and the physical world God created?

Your Short Paper Assignment should be presented in current APA format. It should be 1,250 to 1,750 words in length, not including title page, footnotes/endnotes, abstract, or bibliography.

**Note:** Your assignment will be checked for originality via the Turnitin plagiarism tool.

## Assignment — Topic & Sources Chosen
**Topic:** Digital healthcare infrastructure and macroeconomic policy outcomes in Mexico — a direct precursor to the dissertation's digital health infrastructure / emerging-economies angle.

- **Academic article:** Lomelí Vanegas, L. (2020). The health economy in Mexico. *CEPAL Review, 132*, 181–193.
- **Popular press article:** McDonald, T., Touchton, M., Knaul, F. M., Arreola-Ornelas, H., & Frenk, J. (2023, November 14). The rise and fall of Seguro Popular: Mexico's health care odyssey. *Think Global Health*.

**Epistemological framing applied:** Popper's falsifiability (academic article's reliance on OECD statistics), Kuhn's paradigm shifts (Seguro Popular's rise, 2020 dismantling, and resulting anomalous crisis), and Feyerabend's epistemological anarchism (ad-hoc political solutions like importing Cuban doctors).

**Research questions developed:**
1. To what extent does implementing a centralized digital health registry correlate with a reduction in out-of-pocket healthcare expenditures for marginalized populations in emerging Latin American economies?
2. How does the standardization of clinical metric capture within a fragmented national health system impact the efficiency of public health funding allocations over a ten-year period?

**Dissertation tie-in:** This short paper is the earliest coursework precursor to the dissertation's core research question — it applies the same "digital infrastructure → measurable macroeconomic/health outcomes" logic to a specific country case (Mexico) nearly six weeks before the Week 4 "Leapfrog Advantage & AI Integration" framing was finalized.
