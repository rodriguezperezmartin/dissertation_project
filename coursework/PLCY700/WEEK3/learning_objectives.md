Overview

Doctoral-level thinking requires the ability to critique the reasoning and arguments of others. In this module, you will begin to practice critiquing arguments. You will also begin to consider proper and effective writing style.

Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this module, you will be able to:

Evaluate the necessary elements in an academic book review.
Identify logical fallacies in research.
Apply levels of measurement to concepts and operational definitions.
Differentiate between inductive and deductive reasoning.
