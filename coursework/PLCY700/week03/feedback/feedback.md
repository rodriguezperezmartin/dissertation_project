## Concluding Remarks
> By reflecting on a book review and analyzing it carefully, you have helped to prepare for an assignment of writing a book review later in this course. As you prepare your own book review, think of what you liked or did not like about the book review you read for this assignment. A book review of an academic book should consider the content and relevance of a book, how it fits into the scholarship of its field, and what it contributes or does not contribute to the area of scholarship it addresses. A book review should highlight not only the strengths of the book, but also any shortcomings as well. Academics use book reviews often to help them decide whether to read a book, so think of what you would want to know if you were considering reading the book you are reviewing. I hope you found this exercise useful.

> God bless,

## Key Takeaways / Evaluation Criteria Signaled
- A strong academic book review addresses: **content/relevance**, **fit within the scholarship of its field**, and **contribution (or lack thereof)** to that field.
- Reviews must balance **strengths and shortcomings** — not purely descriptive or purely positive.
- Purpose-driven framing: write as if helping a fellow academic decide whether the book is worth reading.
- This discussion is explicitly scaffolding for a later **graded book review assignment** — the approved book selected here should be the one used in that later assignment.
