## Book Review Assignment — Book Selection
For the Module 6 assignment, the selected book is:

**Weiss, C. H. (1991). *Organizations for policy analysis: Helping government think*. Sage Publications.**

**Why this book was selected:** its insights into how fragmented government agencies process information and integrate data-driven platforms align directly with the dissertation research on public policy and digital healthcare infrastructure. Reviewing this book both practices the academic book review framework (see [week03/assignments/book_review_discussion.md](../week03/assignments/book_review_discussion.md) for the Week 3 model) and produces foundational literature for the dissertation.
