# Week 4 — Learning Objectives & Study Guide

## Overview
Assessing current academic literature is the first step in creating your own unique contribution to the literature. This week you will learn how to write a basic literature review and how to develop a sound, scientific research question from that literature. You will also consider various influences on policy agendas.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this module, you will be able to:
- Evaluate a body of academic literature in your chosen public policy area of interest.
- Develop a scientific research question from your chosen body of academic literature.
- Identify sources of political power and their influence on policy agendas.
- Compare and contrast distributive, regulatory, and redistributive policies.

## Assignment Instructions (Preliminary — from Dr. Boykin's Week 4 announcement)
- Prepare a **literature review** using **10 academic sources** (peer-reviewed journal articles).
- Topic can be something you might want to research later for the dissertation — **strong opportunity to align this with the dissertation topic** (see [dissertation tie-ins](../course_overview.md#dissertation-tie-ins) below).
- Avoid organizing the review paper-by-paper. Instead, **organize topically** — group articles by theme so the reader understands what the research says about a particular area, where sources agree/disagree, and how they differ in methodology and conclusions.
- Use APA in-text citations throughout. Reference guide: [Liberty University APA Writing Guide](https://libguides.liberty.edu/c.php?g=1036190&p=7529654)
