### [Stone, 2012] Decisions; Inducements
- **Citation (APA 7):** Stone, D. (2012). Decisions; Inducements. In *Policy paradox: The art of political decision making* (3rd ed., pp. 248–288). W. W. Norton & Company.
- **Source type:** book chapters (Ch. 10 & 11)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-26
- **Theme(s):** Rational-choice critique, cost-benefit/risk-benefit analysis, decision trees, policy inducements, perverse incentives, goal displacement, power/symbolic politics
- **Summary:** Ch. 10 critiques the rational-analytic decision model (CBA, risk-benefit analysis, decision trees) as concealing political choices behind technical objectivity, contrasting it with decision-making in the *polis*. Read for PLCY 700 Week 5. Full notes: [week05/reading_notes.md](../../../coursework/PLCY700/week05/reading_notes.md).
- **Relevance to dissertation:** Indirect — sharpens critique of purely quantitative M&E/incentive metrics in digital health policy design (risk of goal displacement/gaming when tying funding to narrow performance indicators).
