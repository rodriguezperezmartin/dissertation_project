# Program Information — Ph.D. in Public Policy (Economic Policy Cognate), Liberty University

High-level reference notes about the program. See [roadmap/degree_plan.md](../roadmap/degree_plan.md) for the full course sequencing and timeline.

## Program Summary
- **Degree:** Ph.D. in Public Policy, Economic Policy Cognate
- **Total Credits:** 60 c.h.
- **Structure:** Core Courses (18 c.h.) → Research Methods & Tools (15 c.h.) → Economic Policy Cognate (9 c.h.) → Dissertation Courses (18 c.h.)
- **Continuous enrollment** required once in dissertation phase (Fall/Spring/Summer)
- **Time limit:** 7 years from admission

## Graduation Requirements
- 60 credit hours completed
- Max 50% transfer credit (with approval)
- 3.0 GPA minimum; no grade below B- counts
- Successful completion of Research Concept (PLCY 885)
- Successful dissertation defense (PLCY 990)
- Degree Completion Application filed in final semester
